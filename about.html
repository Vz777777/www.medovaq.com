Who We Are

A Legacy of Precision.
Over 15 Years in Medical Manufacturing.

Founded in India, our mission has been to equip global healthcare systems with uncompromised precision and reliability.

Our Foundation

Medovaq is a leading ISO 13485 certified manufacturer and exporter of premium medical supplies in India. We were born from a singular vision: to democratize access to high-precision surgical tools. Founded in 2008 in the industrial heart of India, our journey began in a modest 2,000 sq. ft. facility focused exclusively on high-grade injection systems, IV cannulas, and disposable syringes.

Our founder, a veteran biomedical engineer, recognized a critical gap in the global market: the "Reliability Gap." While high-end Western instruments were often cost-prohibitive for many emerging healthcare systems, affordable alternatives frequently lacked the necessary precision for critical care. Medovaq was founded to bridge that gap—combining Indian manufacturing efficiency with uncompromising international quality standards.


The Evolution

By 2015, we expanded our manufacturing lines to include advanced respiratory care and blood management systems. This period marked our transition from a local manufacturer to a dedicated global exporter. We invested heavily in State-of-the-art Class 100,000 Clean Rooms and obtained our first major international certifications.

Today, Medovaq operates out of a sprawling multi-wing facility, employing over 200 skilled specialists. We don't just "make" products; we engineer solutions that save lives across 30+ countries, from the busiest hospitals in Europe to remote clinics in Southeast Asia.


Reprocessing & Clinical Care

Our instruments are engineered to survive the rigors of high-volume sterile processing departments (SPD). Below are the validated reprocessing parameters for Medovaq AISI-grade steel:

  • Steam Autoclave: Fully compatible with 134°C (273°F) cycles at 2.1 bar for 15 minutes.
  • ETO Sterilization: Validated for Ethylene Oxide exposure with 100% degassing clearance.
  • Metallurgical Stability: Surgical-grade hardening ensures zero deformation or "pitting" over 100+ cycles.
  • Passivation: All surfaces are chemically passivated to ASTM A967 standards for maximum corrosion resistance.
