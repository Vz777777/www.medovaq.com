Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are core commitments of Medovaq's global B2B operations.

1. Introduction

Medovaq ("we," "our," "us") takes the protection of your business and personal data seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your information when you browse our website, download our product catalogs, or submit inquiries for wholesale procurement.


2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to satisfy business relations and bulk quotes, including:

  • Contact Details: Name, professional email address, phone number, and physical office location.
  • Business Profile: Organization name, registration parameters, target purchase volumes, and delivery destinations.
  • Technical Metrics: IP address, browser metadata, operating system data, and user interaction signals (monitored via Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity).

3. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

We process your data strictly under the following mandates:

  • B2B Quote Requests: To fulfill business quote requests, verify company registration, and draft CIF/FOB export pricing.
  • Analytics Optimization: To monitor system loading performance, identify Core Web Vitals issues, and optimize user navigation patterns.
  • Regulatory Compliance: To satisfy export verification parameters and customs control guidelines.

4. Data Protection & Safety Measures

Medovaq integrates industry-standard encryption controls, SSL certifications, and clean cleanroom access security schemas to prevent data loss or unauthorized leaks. All commercial communications are retained strictly within secure administrative environments.


5. Cookies & Tracking Controls

We utilize essential functional cookies to coordinate site operations and analytical tracking. You can configure your browser preferences to reject non-essential tracking cookies at any time. Non-essential cookies are governed under our global GDPR and CCPA compliant schemas.

If you have any questions or data concerns regarding our global compliance frameworks, please contact our compliance desk at:
